Use Case 3 — Academic Risk Classification

A university wants to identify students at academic risk using multiple indicators. This is a multi-feature classification problem solved with a Ridge Classifier.

  • Independent variables: Study Hours, Attendance, Previous Grade, Assignments Completed
  • Target variable: Academic Risk (0 = Low Risk, 1 = High Risk)
  • Model: Ridge Classifier
